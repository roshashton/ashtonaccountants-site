These Terms explain the basis on which we provide accounting, taxation, bookkeeping, payroll, compliance and advisory services. Our work in relation to self-managed superannuation funds is limited to accounting, taxation and compliance services. Where that advice is required, you should obtain advice from an appropriately licensed adviser or solicitor.
